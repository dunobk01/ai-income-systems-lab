# SEO content plan — ai-income-systems.com

Generated from a Semrush check (US database) run against the published domain.

## Where the site stands today

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Organic keywords | 1 |
| Est. organic traffic | ~0/mo |
| Only ranking keyword | `n8n tool` — position 58, 320/mo, on `/tools/n8n` |

Read: the domain has essentially no organic footprint yet, but the one thing
Google *has* noticed is the n8n tool page. That is the thread to pull.

| Keyword | Volume | KD | Competition | Verdict |

Deliberately skipped: `n8n` (201,000/mo) and other head terms owned by n8n.io
itself. Ranking for a vendor's brand name is not a realistic first move.

## Strategy

Build an **n8n topic cluster** first, because that is the only place the domain
already has traction and the competition scores are unusually soft. Then bridge
that authority into the money/side-hustle terms, which are commercially closer
to the product but harder to rank cold.

```text
        /tools/n8n  (already ranking #58)
              |
   +----------+-----------+
   |                      |
what-is-n8n        n8n-workflows-for-solo-operators
   |                      |
   +----------+-----------+
              |
   ai-automation-for-small-business   (commercial intent)
              |
   +----------+-----------+
   |                      |
how-to-make-money      ai-side-hustles
 -with-ai-no-experience  -around-full-time-job
```

Every post links up to `/tools/n8n`, sideways to its cluster siblings, and down
to `/free` (lead magnet) and `/pricing`.

Each has a hand-written `seo_title` (< 60 chars) and `seo_description`

## What to do next

1. **Wait 4–6 weeks**, then re-run the Semrush domain check. Expect `what is
   n8n` to surface first — it has the widest gap between volume and difficulty.
2. **Add depth, not breadth.** Two more n8n posts (`n8n vs zapier`,
   `n8n self hosting`) beat five posts across unrelated topics.
3. **Point Pinterest at the posts**, not just the product pages. Pinterest is
   already sending traffic and these are far more shareable than /pricing.
4. **Don't chase `how to make money with ai` directly** until the cluster has
   backlinks. KD 72 with zero domain authority is a wasted post.
